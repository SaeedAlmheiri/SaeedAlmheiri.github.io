PhD student in Natural Language Processing focused on Role-Aware Large Language Models (RA-LLMs) for secure, policy-conditioned, and contextualized LLMs in multilingual organizational settings, under the supervision of Dr. [Fajri Koto](https://www.fajrikoto.com/) and Professor [Tim Baldwin](https://eltimster.github.io/www/). Before that, I received my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Arizona State University.

My research interest lie in large language models (LLMs) Alignment, Access Control, User-Preference, Multilingual and Cultural-NLP
